Uses the Web Serial API. The port, baud, data bits, stop bits, parity and hardware flow control take effect when you click Connect. Line termination and Send Line append the chosen terminator to your text. RX is shown as text by default (toggle Hex RX to view bytes hex-encoded). Everything stays in your browser — nothing leaves the page.
