🌳 GATT Browser

Connect to browse services & characteristics

Uses the Web Bluetooth API, which requires a secure context (https or localhost) and a browser that exposes it. Some Chromium builds (including the Canonical snap) ships with Web Bluetooth disabled by default, in which case this page can't pair devices. Fix: launch Chromium with --enable-features=WebBluetooth, or use Google Chrome. Choose a device to pair; the GATT tree lists its services, characteristics and their properties. Read/poll values, write bytes or text, and subscribe to notifications. UUIDs are resolved against the Bluetooth SIG assigned numbers where known. Everything runs in your browser — nothing leaves the page.
